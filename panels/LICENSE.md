The grant above covers the original panel design in this directory — layout,
typography, branding, cutout geometry and artwork by Madelyn Yeary.

It does **not** cover the tiled seamless silkscreen pattern that fills the
between-screws band of the blank faceplates in `blank-*hp/`. That geometry
originates from [pattern.monster](https://pattern.monster/) and remains subject
to pattern.monster's own licence. It is included here under a commercial licence
held by Madelyn Yeary, which permits its use in end-products such as these
panels — it does **not** sublicense the patterns onward. Nothing in the CC
BY-NC-SA 4.0 grant above gives you permission to extract, redistribute, remix or
otherwise reuse that pattern geometry; for that you need your own licence from
pattern.monster.

Everything else in the blank panels — the board outline, mounting holes, screw
strips and `maddie synths` branding — is covered by the grant above as normal.

The pattern source tiles themselves are not distributed with this repository;
see `scripts/panels/tools/blank-patterns/index.md`.
